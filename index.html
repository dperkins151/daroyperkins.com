About

I started as a solar crew lead and became the youngest crew leader in company history at SolarCity (later Tesla). Fourteen years later, I'm superintending commercial rooftop solar — running crews, megawatts, and deadlines.

Somewhere along the way I started writing software for the problems the field actually has: time tracking that doesn't lie, daily logs that write themselves, closeout docs that survive an audit. The office builds software for the office. I build it for the roof.
