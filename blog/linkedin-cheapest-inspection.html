The Cheapest Inspection Is the One You Can Do From the Ground

Nov 2026 · LinkedIn

The cheapest inspection is the one you can do from the ground.

I was told recently to just bend down and look for the wires.

That's one way to run a job. It's also how you miss what's actually wrong.

Here's the math nobody puts in the budget meeting: a thermal drone or a telescopic inspection camera isn't an expense line. It's avoided cost.

One thermal flight over a finished array finds the hot connector, the dead string, the diode failing under load — in an hour, from the ground, before the punch walk. The alternative is a crew on the roof for a day, hunting blind, or worse: finding it at commissioning, under schedule pressure, with the owner's rep standing there.

A pole camera reads a nameplate, a torque mark, a wire tucked where it shouldn't be — without a ladder, without a lift, without putting a body where it doesn't need to be.

Every one of those finds is a truck roll that never happens. A callback that never gets scheduled. A warranty fight that never starts.

Companies track tool budgets to the dollar and then bleed ten times the money on rework, return trips, and guesswork. The tools aren't the cost. Not having them is.

Buy the tool once. Skip the return trip forever.
