The Cheapest Truck Roll Is the One You Never Have to Make

Nov 2026 · LinkedIn

The cheapest truck roll is the one you never have to make.

Every return trip to a site is a receipt for something that got skipped the first time. A connector not fully seated. Torque never verified. A string nobody meggered before it got covered up. Labels that don't match the drawings. Photos nobody took.

None of it is big in the moment. All of it is expensive later — another mobilization, another crew day burned driving back to fix a five-minute miss.

What "right the first time" looks like on my sites:

  • Torque it, mark it, move on.
  • Test it before you cover it. Once it's buried or closed up, the retest costs ten times more.
  • Label it like the next tech is you in six months. It usually is.
  • Photos before close-up, every time.
  • Punch the work while the crew is still standing there, not from a report two weeks later.

Do it right, document it, and the only reason you come back to a site is the next phase of work.
